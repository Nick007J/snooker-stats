Win chances from the scoreboard

How often the player leading by the given frame score went on to win the match; only scores seen in at least 20 matches.

First to a big break

How often the player who first made a break of at least the given size in a frame went on to win that frame.

Breaking off

How often the player who broke off won the frame, in all frames and in deciding frames. Matches whose break-off is on record: the old WST live scoring (2016–23), live captures, Sportradar timelines and snookerscores sheets. Players' figures are in the Break-off view of the Players page.

Needing snookers

How often a player who needed snookers (behind by more than the points left) and still scored again in the frame, from a pot or a foul by the opponent, went on to win it, by the most snookers needed. Frames with every ball potted on record only (live scoring, Sportradar timelines, snookerscores sheets), mostly from 2023 on.

The colour with the reds

Which colour went down after a red, of every colour taken with a red and of the first one in a break. Same frames as above. Players' shares are in the Potting view of the Players page.
